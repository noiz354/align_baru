# Operations runbook (planned)

No live service exists. Before launch assign on-call owner and service SLOs. Initial procedures: (1) elevated 5xx: inspect deploy/change, traces and DB saturation; rollback app if safe. (2) media failures: distinguish CDN/origin/signature expiry from missing object; never make bucket public as workaround. (3) upload safety incident: disable intake, quarantine affected batch, preserve audit evidence, notify security/legal owner. (4) suspected credential exposure: revoke/rotate, invalidate sessions, inspect redacted audit trails. (5) DB incident: stop risky writes, assess replica/PITR, execute approved recovery with RPO/RTO owner. (6) restore: isolated restore, integrity checks, controlled failover and communication. Do not run destructive commands without change approval/backups. Record timeline, impact, mitigations and follow-up tasks; keep evidence access restricted.
