## 2. The ten questions this review had to answer

| # | Question | Answer | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Can a dropped connection stop a sale? | No. Cash, shift start, location report, expenses, counts and closing all work offline; only digital payment creation, loyalty identification and reward redemption require connectivity, by design | `OFFLINE.md`, ADR-0017/0033, NFR-OFFLINE-001/002 |
| 2 | Can the system ever show unverified money as received? | No. `PAID` requires verified evidence or a recorded reconciliation; static QRIS is `PENDING_VERIFICATION` and labelled "Menunggu verifikasi"; verified and unverified amounts are separate fields everywhere | INV-06 + ARC-02, ADR-0012/0033, `PAYMENTS.md`, `docs/payments/QRIS.md` |
| 3 | Can a price change rewrite history? | No. Sale lines store immutable unit-price snapshots; totals derive from snapshots; recomputation must reproduce stored totals exactly | INV-01/INV-08, ADR-0008/0010, `PRICING.md`, `SALES.md` |
| 4 | Can a field expense be used to facilitate an irregular payment? | No. Recording is neutral (`UNVERIFIED_FIELD_EXPENSE`), no recipient/authority/purpose fields exist, no workflow accelerates or hides such payments, flags are record-level patterns reviewed by humans with no automatic consequence | ADR-0027, PRD §9.2, `docs/finance/EXPENSE-REVIEW.md`, FR-EXPENSE-003/012 |
| 5 | Are operators surveilled? | No. Location exists only as explicit shift-bounded reports; no background capture, no timers, no third-party location SDK; `tools/check-stubs.mjs` fails the build if geolocation APIs appear outside the report module | ADR-0007, NFR-PRIVACY-003/004, INV-11 + ARC-06 |
| 6 | Can a stock variance accuse someone? | No. Variance is neutral ("selisih"), `UNKNOWN` is always available, escalation needs two humans and a recorded conclusion, and no status/pay/schedule effect is reachable from variance | ADR-0030, `docs/finance/STOCK-VARIANCE.md`, FR-STOCK-007 |
| 7 | Is recognition gameable or unfair? | Reduced to an acceptable level: multi-factor candidate inputs, no revenue-only measure, normalisation for traffic/duration/day/closures/stock, sample-size gates, published weights, human review, appeal path, no automatic penalty | ADR-0029, `docs/product/OPERATOR-RECOGNITION.md`, FR-PERF-002/004/005/009 |
| 8 | Are duplicates and replays safe? | Yes, structurally: `Idempotency-Key` on every mutation, client UUIDv7 aliases unique per organisation, per-record sync outcomes, duplicate-aware constraints (reward redemption, closing, sale) | ADR-0013, INV-07/INV-14/INV-15, `API.md` §0 |
| 9 | Is every money decision explainable later? | Yes: append-only audit in the same transaction, mandatory reasons for corrections/voids/overrides/reconciliations, immutable accepted closings, shift reconstruction endpoint | ADR-0026/0034, FR-AUDIT-001..008, INV-09/INV-10 |
| 10 | Can one small team operate this? | Yes, by construction: one deployable, one database (system of record + job queue + read models), no broker, no cache, reversible migrations, runbooks RB-01…RB-15 | ADR-0001/0003/0018/0024, `DEPLOYMENT.md`, `RUNBOOK.md` |

## 4. Failure-model review

The ten most likely operational failures were walked through end-to-end (`ARCHITECTURE.md` §10):
device offline, device storage full/corrupt, server unreachable, database failover, worker down,
provider unavailable, object storage down, clock skew, partial batch failure, duplicate callback.
Each has a defined, safe behaviour; none of them can silently produce a money state. The one accepted
asymmetry: an offline device that dies before syncing loses locally recorded data — the mitigation is
explicit (queue durability, per-record state, backup question at handover), not a promise that cannot
be kept.

## 5. Residual risks accepted (with owners)

| # | Residual risk | Why accepted | Owner | Revisit trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-1 | Static-QRIS verification is manual, so revenue recognition lags | Provider onboarding is commercial, and faking success is unacceptable | Finance | Dynamic QRIS slice, or backlog SLA breach for 2 weeks |
| R-2 | Offline device failure can lose unsynced records | Physics of local storage; mitigated by durability and visible state | Ops | A second loss incident in the pilot |
| R-3 | Low-end devices may not support Web Push | In-app alerts are the primary channel by design | Ops | Push adoption becomes necessary for an SLA |
| R-4 | Location reports depend on operator behaviour | Surveillance was refused deliberately; coverage gaps are handled by prompts and supervisor follow-up | Ops | Coverage metric below target with no other cause |
| R-5 | Normalisation needs data volume to be fair | Sample gates suppress thin periods instead of guessing | People | Fewer than 8 shifts per operator per month in the pilot |
| R-6 | Manual reconciliation cost grows with volume | Accepted for the pilot; automated only after provider integration is verified | Finance | Verification backlog > 1 day of work |
| R-7 | Turbopack service-worker friction (Serwist) | The offline shell is a VS-16 concern and the stack is pinned | Platform | VS-16 proves unworkable in the current bundler |
| R-8 | Audit storage growth | Bounded by retention jobs and field minimisation | Platform | Storage cost or query latency breach |

## 6. Things deliberately **not** built (and why that is a feature)

Kafka, Redis, GraphQL, microservices, Kubernetes, native apps, continuous GPS, live operator maps,
fraud scores, automated discipline, loyalty point algorithms, settlement/accounting logic, WhatsApp
integrations, production dashboards, push-driven money states, and any notification path that a money
state depends on. Each rejection is recorded with its reason in `docs/research/STACK-2026.md` §3 or in
the relevant ADR, so a future engineer can reopen it with new evidence rather than by accident.

## 8. Verdict

The design is **coherent enough to build without renegotiating decisions mid-slice**, and honest about
what it does not know: it refuses to invent payment success, refuses to track people, refuses to
accuse them, and refuses to let a leaderboard decide who is good. The first implementation task is
`T-SHIFT-001` (VS-3, Start Shift) and it is **not** started by this phase.

The next action after this review is a human one: record Gate-9 sign-off from the Fintech and
Privacy owners, then open `T-SHIFT-001` with `AGENTS.md` as the working contract.
