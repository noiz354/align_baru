Wireframe: Failed image / completion card / offline banner

Grayscale blocks and region labels only. No colour, type, or scale decision is made at this tier.

Annotations

  • The failed-image placeholder is per page, not per chapter (§14). The rest of the chapter stays fully usable because each page has independent state, and one automatic retry runs after 500ms before the manual button matters.
  • Retry is bounded. Cache-busted re-requests stop after two attempts and the button stays for manual action. No loop, no storm.
  • Three distinct failures in a session raise a banner, and that banner suppresses the automatic retries (§14).
  • The completion card has three different endings (§13) and the difference is the state, not the wording: a published next chapter, the end of the series, and a next chapter that exists but is still draft.
  • Auto-advance is visible and cancellable: a 1.5s countdown that any input cancels. A countdown with no visible state is a trap.
  • The offline banner is honest (§15): it says images may not load, and it does not imply an offline download exists. There is deliberately none (NO-6).
  • Progress keeps being written while images fail (§15): the position does not depend on the artwork, so a storage outage never costs a reader their place.
