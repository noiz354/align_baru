Wireframe: Admin: upload pages

Route /admin/uploads · Tier 1, structure only · Source admin-workflow.md §5, §6, EC-UP-01…10

Grayscale blocks and region labels only. No colour, type, or scale decision is made at this tier.

Annotations

  • The job state machine is the screen. admin-workflow.md §6: queued → validating → processing → ready, with failed terminal. Transitions are forward-only and each one is audited.
  • Failure is a typed, actionable sentence, never a bare code: what to do about it, and which pages failed, capped at 20 listed.
  • A failing job is terminal — no automatic retry, because retrying flaky storage would mask the bug. The curator re-uploads (admin-workflow.md §5).
  • One active job per chapter. A second submit is 409 job-busy; the screen offers to watch the running job rather than to submit again.
  • Client checks are advisory. Size, count, and format hints appear before upload, but the server is authoritative (NFR-SEC-007) and its typed errors are what the curator acts on.
  • Ready is not published. J-4 step 5 gives two distinct actions — View chapter (a draft preview) and Publish — and step 6 closes the loop by opening the published chapter in the reader.
  • Replace-or-fail is stated before the click (J-5), including the promise that a failed re-ingest leaves the current pages in place.

Failure variants a curator can act on

  • "Some images could not be read" — pages 7, 12 and 19 failed. Fix and re-upload; no re-ingest is needed because nothing was committed.
  • "The archive expands beyond the safe limit" — re-compress or split the chapter.
  • "Password-protected archives are not supported" — re-package without encryption.
  • "No images found in the archive" — the archive holds directories only.
  • "A job for this chapter is already running" — watch the running job instead.
  • "Processing exceeded 15 minutes and was stopped" — re-upload.
