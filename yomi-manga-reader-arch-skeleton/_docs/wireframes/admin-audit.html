Wireframe: Admin: audit log

Route /admin/audit · Tier 1, structure only · Source admin-workflow.md §8, EC-ADM-06

Grayscale blocks and region labels only. No colour, type, or scale decision is made at this tier.

Annotations

  • The log is append-only and immutable (admin-workflow.md §8). There is no delete or edit path, and the interface does not imply one.
  • Every row names the actor, the action, the target, and the time. A deleted actor renders as <deleted> rather than as a blank cell.
  • Before and after are capped at 2 KB per field and truncation is marked (EC-ADM-06). A full synopsis is never in the log, and a marked truncation is better than a silently short value.
  • Filters are by target kind and id, with a cursor list rather than page numbers — the log grows without bound and retention is at least a year.
