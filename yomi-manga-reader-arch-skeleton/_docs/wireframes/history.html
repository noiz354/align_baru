Wireframe: History

Route /history · Tier 1, structure only · Source PRD FR-LIBRARY-*, EC-ADM-03

Grayscale blocks and region labels only. No colour, type, or scale decision is made at this tier.

Annotations

  • History is sessions, not a progress log (J-2 step 5). A session ends after five minutes idle (INT-PROG-002), so the list stays short and readable instead of becoming a wall of page events.
  • Each row is resumable and states its own position and deepest page, because a reader needs to know whether a row is worth opening before opening it.
  • An orphaned chapter renders as "Unavailable chapter" (EC-ADM-03) rather than vanishing. A curator's soft-delete must not silently rewrite a reader's history.
  • Retention is stated, not silent. Rows age out; the screen says so once, in a sentence, instead of leaving a reader wondering where last month went.
