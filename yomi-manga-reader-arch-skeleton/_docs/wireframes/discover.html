Annotations

  • Anonymous home is the catalog. J-1 step 1: / renders a catalog preview for signed-out visitors and the LCP element is the first cover card (NFR-PERF-001). This page is that view.
  • Card content is fixed by FR-CATALOG-005: cover, title, status, latest chapter. Card accessible names are descriptive — "{title} — manga, {status}" (ACCESSIBILITY.md §2) — which is why status is text in the card, not only a badge colour (NFR-A11Y-008).
  • Filters are an aside landmark (ACCESSIBILITY.md §2), not a bare div, and they become a disclosure under the small breakpoint rather than shrinking into unreadable slivers.
  • The empty state is not a blank grid. J-1 failure branch: an empty catalog says so and offers the next step. See error-states.
