What to try

M cycles the mode. Vertical to single to double and back. Notice that the page you were on does not move, and that the zoom resets when you cross into or out of double — that is the documented rule, not a bug.

D flips the direction. The column reorders so the reading-start page is at the top, the arrow keys swap meaning, the tap zones swap sides, the spread pairs change, and the indicator recomputes from displayIndex — while the physical page number stays exactly where it was. Watch the indicator when you flip.

End reaches the reading end. Wait a second and the completion card appears with a 1.5 second countdown. Press Space to cancel it. Continue to Chapter 13, press End again, and you get the "not published yet" variant. Chapter 14 is the end of the series.

Turn on "Show the tap zones". The zone labelled "next page" is the start third in right to left and the end third in left to right.

Swipe in single or double mode. Release past a quarter of the box, or flick it fast, and the page turns. Release early and it animates back with no state change.

Switch on "Stop failing page 7" off, then go there. In vertical mode, use Home then scroll, or switch to single and use Home then navigate. The failed page holds its exact height, so nothing shifts around it.

B hides the chrome, and tapping the centre of the page brings it back. F requests fullscreen on the reader container. Esc exits fullscreen, then closes a dialog, then hides the chrome, in that order.

Turn the whole page off. Every action above has a visible, focusable button: the rail and the bars are the real interface, and the tap zones and swipes sit on top of them. Press Tab from the canvas and nothing is more than a few stops away.
