Error, not found, and empty

Each state below names the cause, says what the reader can do, and offers somewhere to go. None is a status code on its own, and none is a blank main. Focus moves to the state on entry, and it is announced.

Error boundary

src/app/error.tsx · EC-XX-04

Something went wrong on this page

Cause. The catalog could not be read from the database. Nothing you did caused this, and it is not your account.

What you can do. Try again, or go back to the catalog. Your reading position is saved on this device and is not affected.

Go to the catalog

Not found

src/app/not-found.tsx · EC-XX-06, EC-ADM-02

This page is not here

Cause. The link points at a page that does not exist, or at a title a curator removed. A removed title answers this way on purpose — the two are the same answer for a reader, and saying which one it was would tell them about content they are not entitled to know exists.

What you can do. Search the catalog, or go back to where you were.

Search the catalog Go to the catalog

Chapter unavailable

EC-RDR-02 no pages · EC-RDR-08 deleted mid-session

This chapter is not available

Cause. The chapter was removed by the curator, or it has no pages yet.

What you can do. There is no reader here, and that is deliberate: an empty reader shell with dead controls is worse than an honest stop. Open the series to see the chapters that are available.

Back to the series Go to the catalog

Permission

admin-workflow.md §1 · a 403 page, not a sign-in redirect

You do not have access to the admin panel

Cause. Your account is signed in and is not an administrator. Sending you to a sign-in form you already passed would be a dead end, so this is a permission answer.

Go to the catalog

Empty states

Informative, and each one offers the next action

empty

The collection is being prepared — check back soon

The catalog has no titles yet. Content is added by the curator, so there is nothing to search and no reason to sign in.

empty

Nothing in your library yet

Titles you start appear here with the page you stopped on.

Browse the catalog
empty

No titles match “archivist”

Nothing in the catalog has that word in a title or an alias. Two creators have it in their name.

Browse the catalog
empty

No bookmarks yet

A bookmark is per page, so you can come back to one panel later.

No manga yet — create your first title

A new instance starts empty. Create a title, add a chapter, upload its pages, then publish — and check the chapter in the reader before you call it done.
