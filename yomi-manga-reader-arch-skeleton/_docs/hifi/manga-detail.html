Synopsis

The second-year archivist is handed a ledger she did not request, a desk nobody wants, and a question about shelf nine that the catalogue says has never been asked.

What follows is a slow novel about repair work: fixing footnotes, re-binding ledgers, and the small social cost of being the person who notices a mislaid page and says so out loud.
