Lo-fi design set

Real layout, real type scale, real spacing rhythm, and nothing else. The palette is deliberately grayscale and depth comes from borders, so proportion and density can be judged on their own. The next tier adds colour and depth; the tier after that makes it interactive.

What tier 2 settles

  • Type scale

    12 / 13 / 15 / 16 for body and labels, 24 and 32 for headings, 40 for a manga title. Body copy never drops below 16; 15 and 13 are for labels and metadata only.

  • Spacing

    A 4px grid used as 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64. Nothing sits on a value that is not on the grid.

  • Depth

    One hairline border token and surface tint. No shadows anywhere, so an element never competes with the content for attention.

  • Density

    Catalog cards at six across on desktop and two at 320px. The admin table holds eight columns and scrolls horizontally inside its own container rather than widening the page.

  • Touch targets

    Every control is at least 44 by 44, including the reader chrome and the small text buttons in the chapter list.
