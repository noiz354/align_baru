Error, not found, and empty states

Every state below has a cause, a next action, and a link home. None of them is a status code on its own, and none of them is a blank main.

Error boundary

src/app/error.tsx · the RSC error boundary, EC-XX-04

Something went wrong on this page

Cause. the catalog could not be read from the database. Nothing you did caused this.

What you can do. try again, or go back to the catalog. Your reading position is saved on this device and is not affected.

Go to the catalog

Focus lands on this region on entry and it is announced.

Not found

src/app/not-found.tsx · EC-XX-06, EC-ADM-02

This page is not here

Cause. the link points at a page that does not exist, or at a title that was removed. A removed title answers the same way on purpose.

What you can do. search the catalog, or go back to where you were.

Search the catalogGo to the catalog

Focus lands on this region on entry and it is announced.

Chapter unavailable

EC-RDR-02 a chapter with no pages · EC-RDR-08 deleted mid-session

This chapter is not available

Cause. the chapter was removed by the curator, or it has no pages yet.

What you can do. there is no reader here, and that is deliberate. Open the series to see the chapters that are available.

Back to the seriesGo to the catalog

Focus lands on this region on entry and it is announced.

Permission

admin-workflow.md §1 · a 403, not a sign-in redirect

You do not have access to the admin panel

Cause. your account is signed in but is not an administrator. This is a permission answer, not a request to sign in again.

What you can do. go back to the catalog. If you need curator access, ask the instance operator.

Go to the catalog

Focus lands on this region on entry and it is announced.

Empty states

empty

The collection is being prepared — check back soon

The catalog has no titles yet. Content is added by the curator, so there is nothing to search and nothing to sign in for.

empty

Nothing in your library yet

Titles you start appear here with the page you stopped on.

Browse the catalog
empty

No titles match “archivist”

Try a creator or a tag. Two creators in this catalog have titles with “archivist” in them.

Browse the catalog
empty

No bookmarks yet

A bookmark is per page, so you can come back to one panel later.

empty

No manga yet — create your first title

A new instance starts empty. Create a title, add a chapter, upload its pages, then publish.
