Reader states

The reader's own states: a per-page failure, the offline banner, and the three different completion cards. Each one states what happened and what to do next.

Offline banner

Offline — some images may not load. Pages you have already read stay readable and your position keeps being saved on this device.

The banner never implies an offline download exists. There is deliberately none.

The other reader notices

  • Progress sync failed

    Your position is saved on this device and will be retried. One notice, not one per write.

  • Deep link clamped

    ?page=999 became page 240.

  • Chapter shrank after a re-ingest

    Your position moved to the new last page.

  • Start of reading reached

    A brief no-op cue. The reader never wraps around.
